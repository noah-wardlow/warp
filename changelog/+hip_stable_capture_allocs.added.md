On HIP, allocations made during graph capture now pause the capture and use the plain allocator by default, so captured graphs carry no memory-allocation nodes and replays touch stable addresses. This eliminates a ROCm graph-replay fault class for capture-time allocations (deterministic on non-primary devices, e.g. a captured padded `bsr_set_transpose` faulting on relaunch on `cuda:1`) and makes graphs with capture-time allocations relaunchable; the buffers live for the graph's lifetime. Opt out with `WARP_HIP_STABLE_CAPTURE_ALLOCS=0`.
