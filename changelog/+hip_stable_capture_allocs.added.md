Add opt-in `WARP_HIP_STABLE_CAPTURE_ALLOCS=1` on HIP: allocations made during graph capture pause the capture and use the plain allocator, so captured graphs carry no memory-allocation nodes and replays touch stable addresses. This eliminates an intermittent ROCm graph-replay fault class for capture-time allocations and makes graphs with capture-time allocations relaunchable; the buffers live for the graph's lifetime. Off by default.
